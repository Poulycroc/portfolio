## Context

The very first project with Arkdevel — the company was founded on this project. DediKey is an access control system for physical entry points (front doors, garages, gates, etc.). Through the backoffice, administrators could control who can enter or exit, on specific time slots or not. Users could then call or send an SMS to the gate to gain access.

## Technical Highlights

- Full backoffice for access management (add/ban users, manage gates)
- Access control via phone call, SMS, or RFID badge
- Time-slot-based access scheduling
- Complete IoT management (hardware, SIM cards)
- Entry/exit statistics (who, when, traffic)
- Django backend, NuxtJS frontend, Node services
