ArkDevel is the company I co-founded in 2017 in Namur. GIMI is one of the business applications we designed and built for our clients — custom SaaS tools covering management, reporting and process automation.

- Complete and documented REST API
- Multi-tenant management system
- User interfaces designed for end users, not just developers

## Technical Highlights

- Optimized MySQL database modeling
- Full product cycle: client requirements analysis, direct communication, UI/UX, development, deployment and iterations
