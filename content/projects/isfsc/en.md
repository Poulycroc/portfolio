## Context

ISFSC is a Brussels higher education institution. For 4 months, I had the opportunity to share my passion and deep knowledge of PHP, HTML and WordPress with a new generation of aspiring developers.

## Subjects Taught

- PHP — backend programming, server logic, databases
- HTML — semantic structure, accessibility
- WordPress — custom theme and plugin development, administration

## Teaching Approach

- Planning and delivering interactive hands-on lessons to facilitate understanding of key concepts
- Evaluating student progress and providing constructive feedback to improve understanding and practical skills
- Adapting teaching methods to individual needs and encouraging curiosity for web development
- Keeping my own knowledge up to date with technologies and trends to ensure relevant teaching
