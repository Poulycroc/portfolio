## Context

Afroditi is a crypto project that needed a presentation website online by the next day. The challenge: integrate 6 pages from a mockup in under 24 hours, with multilingual content received progressively throughout the day and evening. Completed in 2024.

## Key Features

- Multilingual support
- 6 pages integrated in under 24 hours
