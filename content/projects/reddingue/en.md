## Context

Red Dingue is a project completed in one day in 2022 for a restaurant owner client. It required a public landing page and a full back-office to manage the restaurant's content and events.

## Key Features

- Restaurant presentation landing page
- CMS back-office for content management
- Event management with table reservations
- Dietary requirements and guest preferences tracking

## Technical Highlights

- Laravel (backend + CMS) + Vue.js (frontend) architecture
- Reservation system with dietary constraint management
- Completed in one day
