## Context

HR Alert is a company specialized in HR newsletters. HrPartner is an internal tool I designed and developed over 4.5 years remotely from Namur, enabling campaign planning and detailed performance analysis.

## Key Features

- Multi-client newsletter campaign management and publication back-office
- Mailchimp statistics visualization with detailed performance analysis
- Campaign scheduling and planning
- Multi-user and role management

## Technical Highlights

- Laravel (backend) + Nuxt.js (frontend) architecture for the complete back-office
- Mailchimp API integration for campaign statistics retrieval and visualization
- Docker orchestration of multiple services (frontend, backend, Mailchimp service) with separation of concerns
- Close collaboration with cross-functional teams to translate business needs into product features
