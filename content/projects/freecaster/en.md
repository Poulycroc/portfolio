## Context

Freecaster is a Nannine-based company specialized in streaming and content management. For just over a year, I actively participated in the complete rebuild of their management application, migrating to a modern stack.

## Key Features

- Stream and multimedia content management
- Complete redesigned admin interface
- Automated CI/CD pipeline for testing and deployment

## Technical Highlights

- Complete application rebuild with Laravel, Vue.js 2, Node.js and SASS
- Robust unit testing for code quality and regression prevention
- Docker for application packaging and deployment across environments
- Continuous integration with Drone CI to automate testing and deployment
