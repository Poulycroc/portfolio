## Context

TheGreenShot is a film and TV production company committed to sustainability. For nearly 5 years as a freelancer, I developed and maintained their admin application — a business tool helping production teams stay organized, control costs and reduce their carbon footprint.

## Key Features

- Timesheet, expense report, purchase order and invoice management
- Real-time salary and budget tracking
- Real-time carbon footprint tracking (individual and team)
- eIDAS-compliant electronic signature
- Open import/export system

## Technical Highlights

- Complete Vue.js application with specialized business components for audiovisual production
- eIDAS-compliant electronic signature integration
- Real-time carbon tracking — aligned with TheGreenShot's sustainability commitment
- Direct collaboration with production teams and department heads to translate their needs into features
