## Context

Club Avantage was a lottery-style platform. Visitors discovered the service through a brochure, visited the landing page and submitted their email. The back-office then managed daily draws with automatic congratulation emails sent to winners. The project was completed in one week at the end of 2022.

## Key Features

- Responsive landing page integrated from a designer mockup
- Full back-office for participant and draw management
- Automated daily draw system with email notifications
- Advanced user role and permission management in the back-office
- Win frequency control system per participant

## Technical Highlights

- Vue.js (frontend) + Lumen (backend) architecture
- Custom-built role and permission management system
- Complex business logic for draw and prize distribution
- Automated email sending integration
