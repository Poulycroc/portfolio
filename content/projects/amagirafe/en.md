## Context

AMA is an educational tool that connects classrooms with other French-speaking classrooms around the world. It helps students aged 3 to 11 develop their openness to the world while strengthening their psychosocial skills. One-week mission in 2023 in collaboration with Cherry Pulpe.

## Key Features

- Platform connecting French-speaking classrooms
- Educational tools adapted for ages 3-11

## Technical Highlights

- Laravel + WordPress (content and language management) + Vue.js architecture
- Debugging and stabilization of existing features
- Collaboration with the Cherry Pulpe team
