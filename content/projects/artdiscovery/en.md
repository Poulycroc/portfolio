## Context

A client with a WordPress art showroom wanted a tool to zoom into specific areas of each artwork. The concept: define points of interest on a painting (e.g. on the Mona Lisa, "top left: the scenery", "center: the smile") and navigate smoothly between these zones across the entire gallery. Completed in 2 days in 2022.

## Key Features

- Progressive smooth zoom between points of interest in each artwork
- Zone definition with descriptive annotations per painting
- Continuous navigation across all gallery artworks
- Ultra-smooth transitions between points of interest

## Technical Highlights

- Custom WordPress plugin
- Zoom and transition animations entirely hand-built, without Three.js or any 3D library
- Coordinate and viewport management for smooth rendering
- Developed without AI assistance
